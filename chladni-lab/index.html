How the model works

The dial changes two parameters, n and m, in Prízrak’s standing-wave model. Two cosine patterns overlap on a gently warped square. Each grain moves downhill in vibration amplitude and collects near a nodal line, where the modeled amplitude crosses zero.

The cyan dots locate sign changes along one horizontal cut. A closed loop can miss that cut entirely. A “quiet” grain is one whose absolute field value falls below 0.08 in model units.

The dial is unitless. A physical plate has its own resonant frequencies, set by its material, shape and mounting. See a real plate in Harvard’s lecture demonstration.
