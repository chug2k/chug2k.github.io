How it works

Three steps to measurable sunshine

"Measurable" is a claim we can defend. We have chosen our words carefully throughout this page.

Tilt & drop

Tilt your head back, pull down the lower lid, and place one drop in the corner of the eye. There will be a stinging sensation. Duration varies by individual; it has never been zero.

Blink it home

Blink gently. Your eye will begin reflex tearing, which flushes a portion of the dose back out. We recommend blinking anyway, as the alternative is worse.

Absorb & retain

Of what remains, a fraction reaches the bloodstream through the nasal mucosa. The rest exits down your cheek. Internal testing suggests the cheek is the primary destination.

The science, disclosed in full

OcuD3 is built on one real anatomical fact and constrained by three real physiological ones. Most supplement companies would not publish this section. We consider that their weakness.

✅ The route is real

Ophthalmic drugs drain via the tear duct and are absorbed systemically — it's why glaucoma drops can lower heart rate. The pathway exists, works, and is used by real medicine every day. For real medicine, the required dose fits in a drop.

⚠️ Solubility

Vitamin D is fat-soluble; the tear film is aqueous. Our lipid emulsion resolves this at the cost of a greasy film across the cornea. Users describe the first nine minutes of their morning as "vaseline-adjacent."

⚠️ Volume

The ocular surface holds approximately 30 microliters. One drop is 50. The overflow — 40% of the dose — never had a chance, and we want to be clear that we knew that when we designed the bottle.

⚠️ Rejection

Concentrated drops sting, and stinging triggers reflex tearing, which flushes the dose. The more vitamin D we put in a drop, the more efficiently your eye removes it. We have not solved this. We do not currently believe it can be solved.

⚖️ Final design verdict

To deliver a full dose through the eye, you would need an oily, eye-hostile, hyper-concentrated solution, applied dozens of times per session, that burns on contact, blurs your vision, and mostly runs down your face — arriving, eventually, at the dose one softgel and a fatty breakfast delivers in two seconds.
