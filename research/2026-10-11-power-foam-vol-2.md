# Power-foam buying guide, Vol. 2 — source ledger

Research cutoff and access date: **October 11, 2026**. Companion article: [October buying guide](../blog/posts/2026-10-11-power-foam-paddle-buying-guide-vol-2.md). Publication dates below are source dates; approval dates and expected shipping dates are explicitly different things.

This is a selective buying guide, not a census or a numerical meta-ranking. No first-hand paddle testing was performed for it. Recommendations synthesize the accessible evidence and state where a court demo or more evidence is needed.

## Method and access

- Searched the web and current YouTube uploads from John Kew, Pickleball Effect and Pickleball Studio, then followed new-model, durability and official-product leads. Discovery extended beyond June's shortlist.
- `yt-dlp` retrieved video metadata, descriptions and upload dates. English auto-captions were retrieved for the September 4 KewCast and June 18 SupaSenpai teardown; relevant passages were read. Other requested caption downloads returned HTTP 429, or no matching caption track. No inaccessible episode is presented as watched or transcribed.
- Raw metadata, caption files and transcript scratch text are outside this repository, under `/private/tmp/power-foam-vol2/`. This ledger contains summaries only. Auto-captions can misrecognize names and numbers; use the linked video for confirmation.
- Reviewer affiliate links/codes and supplied samples are potential incentives. Editorial-independence disclosures do not remove them. Manufacturer pages establish advertised specifications, prices and order status, not independent performance. Reddit reports establish anecdotes, not failure rates.
- All prices are **USD**, checked October 11, excluding taxes, delivery and import costs. Advertised codes were not tested through checkout. “Add to cart,” preorder and backorder are recorded separately; delivery was not verified. Warranty notes summarize product-page wording, not an adjudication of an individual claim.
- Accelerated spin loss, abrasive texture loss and actual court hours are different measurements. Tier labels are John Kew's testing categories, not official certifications. Historical and revised Power/Pop scores are not combined into a ranking.

Existing certifications remain under applicable certification standards. A reviewer's RPM is not interchangeable with this test.
- **USA Pickleball — [Coral Pro entry 13780](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/paddle-list/entry/13780/):** Pass, added **October 1, 2026**, RCD 16MM, hybrid, diamond-infused carbon, Peel Ply. Checked October 11. Retail Ultra Rough/shape mapping remains unresolved.
- **USA Pickleball — [V-Sol Tour Elongated entry 13672](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/paddle-list/entry/13672/):** Pass, added **August 6, 2026**, elongated 16mm. Checked October 11. Does not automatically establish all V-Sol shapes.
- **UPA-A — [approved paddles](https://upaa.unitedpickleball.com/approved-paddles/):** checked October 11. Relevant exact entries: Jelly Bean 2 **992.2T-H/P/U/V**, July 24; Ultré Power 2 **W212.63-U**, May 4; CMD HYB **V0014**, August 20; Coral Pro **RCD/RCD-E/RCD-W**, May 19–29; Perseus Power FX **P16G3FD**, September 22. A third-party approval mirror was a discovery aid, not substituted for the official registry.

## Historical baseline and unresolved questions

- [June 4 guide / Vol. 1](../paddle-buying-guide-2026/index.html) and [older meta-ranking](../v/best-paddles-2026-meta-ranking/index.html): historical recommendations only. Their review totals were not re-audited or inherited. Both remain unchanged by this task.
- No controlled multi-brand core-failure incidence study or general foam lifespan was established. Surface retention cannot stand in for core longevity. Warranty replacements and anecdotes do not provide a denominator.
- September/October evidence is strongest for new value choices, sample-dependent surface wear and the one-paddle 100-game follow-up. Longer production histories for new launches remain absent or inaccessible.
- The inaccessible October 7 discussion could contain relevant newer results; the guide explicitly withholds a conclusion where that matters. No conclusions about fixing Enhance batch two, long-term Power FX life, or delivered CMD/Loco 2 production are invented.
