The written order

What came through?

The script went into the synthesizer. Whisper listened to the resulting audio. Word error rate counts words replaced, missing or added, divided by the number of words in the written order.

How the words are counted

We lowercase English words, replace punctuation with spaces, and align the two word lists using the fewest edits. A change in punctuation alone costs zero. The dispatch score compares the order with Whisper. Comparing the script with Whisper isolates the transcription step.
