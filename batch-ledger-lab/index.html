    How the costing works

    Each lot has an opening quantity and a purchase cost. The share of that lot used in a batch is (in goods + scrap) ÷ opening quantity. Multiply that share by the lot’s cost and round it to a whole cent. Add the lot costs and divide by finished units to get the average.

    A reversal puts back the quantities and the exact cents from the posted entry. This is a materials-only exercise. Labor, tax and selling price are outside the calculation. Each practice sheet can have one posted batch at a time.
