The essays on this site take weeks and occasionally involve illustrated foxes. This is the other thing: short posts, published fast, edited never. Build notes, things I read, opinions I'll probably retract.

What it isn't: a newsletter (no signup form), a content strategy (no calendar), or a personal brand (no professional headshot). There's an RSS feed, because some of us still believe in it.

First real post soon. Or never. That's the beauty of low expectations.
