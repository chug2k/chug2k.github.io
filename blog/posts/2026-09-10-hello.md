The long essays on this site take weeks. This notebook is for everything else: small observations, build notes, things I changed my mind about.

## What goes here

- **Build notes** — what shipped today, what broke, what I learned.
- **Reading marginalia** — a paragraph on something worth your time.
- **Corrections** — when an old page turns out wrong, the fix lands here first.

## How posts get made

Write Markdown in `blog/posts/`, then build:

```
./_scripts/new-post.sh "A working title"  # scaffold
python3 _scripts/build-blog.py            # render
```

> Edit the `.md`, rebuild, commit on a branch, open a PR. No bundler, no CMS.

If you're reading this in RSS: welcome. The feed is at [/feed.xml](/feed.xml) and it's hand-rolled, on purpose.
