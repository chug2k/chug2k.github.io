A print shop wants to stay open until 5:30. The pull request changes the door sign, but it also announces an indigo workshop nobody has approved. Can you get the sign onto staging without sending the announcement to production? The question comes from a 2021 CoderSchool Git guide. The print shop is new. [Try Branch Room.](/branch-room/)

## Four more lessons

**Layout Workbench.** A 48-pixel gap looks right until you widen the shelf. Edit the CSS and check where each piece actually lands at both widths. The exercise revisits Minh’s December 2020 CSS lab. [Arrange the shelf.](/layout-workbench/)

**Taccola.** Rill the jackdaw is looking for a missing concert bell. Two rooms give the same reading. If your query keeps only the first, you’ll miss a clue. This is a new playable mystery built around the companion-led SQL quest from 2023. [Open the SQL notebook.](/taccola-sql/)

**Batch Ledger.** A run of 24 notebooks used twelve misprinted sheets and one torn cover. Trace the cost to the stockroom, then see what happens if that cover goes back on the shelf. [Try the notebook run.](/blog/2026-11-26-what-does-one-notebook-cost/)

**The Chladni Table.** Sand collects along the quiet lines of a vibrating plate. Count the crossings on one simulated plate, predict what the second will show, and save the two patterns side by side. [Compare two plates.](/chladni-lab/)

## Three tools to try

**Subtitle Desk.** One line of dialogue runs into the next. Trim the overlap, listen to the gap as tones, and download the corrected subtitles as an SRT. [Edit a cue sheet.](/subtitle-desk/)

**Tiny Hermes.** Tell a briefing to put blockers first and name their owners. Open a fresh session, compare the two reports, then advance the clock to see exactly what the scheduled run remembered. The lesson uses a local formatter and a simulated clock. [Try the briefing room.](/tiny-hermes/)

**Beatrice Room.** Which paper bridge will hold the most coins? Open the facilitator and learner views in two tabs. Make a prediction, agree on a fair test, then see what made it into the reflection. [Rehearse a session.](/beatrice-room/)

## Two games

**Bubble Wrap Club.** Pop 42 bubbles. A second press on a popped bubble costs you accuracy. The receipt can replay your exact route and give you a souvenir card. [Pop a sheet.](/bubble-wrap-club/)

**Sammy’s Bork-o-Meter 9000.** Sammy is a corgi on neighborhood watch. Record a bark, reload, and undo it when you realize it was the neighbor’s dog. [Work a shift with Sammy.](/sammys-bork-meter/)
