Hi, I’m Charles.

I make small things and write them up.

I’m interested in what happens when you follow an idea a little too far: a lighter music app, an illustrated pickleball book, a robot that keeps making tiny web tools.

This site is where those things end up. The writing covers what I tried, what broke, and what I’d do differently. The projects are things you can read, use, or play.
